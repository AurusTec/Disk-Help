Scope: landing page `site/index.html`. Mode: Persuade.
Audience/job: someone in Goiânia region with something broken, on a phone, wants a trustworthy person fast. Action: start a WhatsApp conversation (+55 62 99870-7878).
Proof/content: flyer service list, since 1998, residential and business, service area Goiânia + ~100 km. No invented testimonials, prices, or warranty terms.
Build path: code-led (no image generation on this machine). Concept roll not run: brand identity is pinned by the flyer; direction below expands it.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the help call itself. Instead of a brochure with a service card grid, the visitor taps what broke and the page writes their WhatsApp message for them. Refuses the category default of stock-photo technician hero plus 3x4 icon cards plus testimonials.

OWN-WORLD: Brand green fields owning whole regions (deep green hero, pale green composer ground), white, red reserved for the one action and the swoosh. Heavy Archivo lettering at expanded width for shouting headlines, italic for the "Se tem um problema," setup, like the flyer. Round green icon badges from the flyer, authored as one stroke family. Service-order ticket (perforated edge, stamped fields) as the component language for the composer.

STORY: I see immediately they fix what I have, in my city; I tap my problem; the message is ready; I send it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Deep green field. Left: "Se tem um problema," italic, then "DÁ UM HELP" at display scale with the red wrench. One line: soluções completas para casa e empresa em Goiânia e região, desde 1998. Primary red button "Chamar no WhatsApp", secondary phone link. Right (desktop): the service ticket already showing a sample message bubble; on mobile it follows directly below.

FORM: Service-order ticket / WhatsApp composer, 1st of ordered list; seed key: none (roll not run, brand-pinned).
