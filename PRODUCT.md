# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step (user's choice).

## Users

Residents and businesses in Goiânia-GO and within ~100 km of the metropolitan region who have something broken or needing service at home or at work (appliance, air conditioning, plumbing, electrical, lock, pests) and want someone trustworthy to come fix it quickly. Most arrive on a phone and want to talk to a person, usually on WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

Disk Help Serviços Auto e Residência is a service provider for homes and businesses. The site exists to make the service range clear and turn a visitor with a problem into a WhatsApp conversation or phone call. Success is a contact started.

## Positioning

Operating since 1998. One number for a wide range of household and business repairs, so the customer does not have to find a different specialist for each problem ("Se tem um problema, DÁ UM HELP").

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Disk Help (full: Disk Help Serviços Auto e Residência). Descriptor: "Prestadora de serviços residencial e empresarial".
- Slogan: "Se tem um problema, DÁ UM HELP". Headline: "Soluções completas para o seu dia a dia."
- Identity from the flyer (`Disk-Help/image.png`): green primary, red accent (swoosh over the "DH" monogram), white ground, house pictogram above the monogram, heavy bold sans lettering, wrench motif. Binding.

## Evidence on Hand

- No testimonials, reviews, client names, prices, warranty terms, response-time figures, photos of the team or address. Do not fabricate any of these.

## Product Principles

1. The shortest path from "something broke" to talking to Disk Help wins.
2. Show the whole service range at a glance; the breadth is the offer.
3. Only claim what the company already claims: since 1998, fast and professional, fair price, service guarantee.
4. Local and personal: Goiânia and region, a real number, a real person.
